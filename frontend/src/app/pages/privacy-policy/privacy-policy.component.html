Privacy Notice: This document outlines the operational privacy policy of AapnoSaath Care regarding how personal information is handled and safeguarded for families in India.

1. Overview & Scope

AapnoSaath Care ("we", "us", or "our") operates an elder-care support, hospital appointment accompaniment, and family coordination service in Vadodara, Gujarat. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, handle, and safeguard personal information submitted by adult children, family members, and elderly parents who use our website and care services.

2. Information We Collect

We strictly limit information collection to what is necessary to fulfill enquiries and coordinate visits:

  • Enquirer / Family Details: Full name, email address, telephone number, relationship to the parent, and contact preferences.
  • Service Location: Locality and PIN code in Vadodara to determine coordinator routing and coverage.
  • Support & Assistance Context: Routine preferences, conversational interests, hospital accompaniment needs, and errand assistance scope.

What we DO NOT collect: We do not collect detailed electronic health records, bank passwords, Aadhaar card photocopies on public forms, or credit card numbers on this website.

3. Consent-Based Family Updates

Visit summaries and check-in reports are shared with nominated family members only with the knowledge, agreement, and comfort of the elderly parent. Parents retain the right to specify boundaries regarding what personal details are communicated.

4. Data Retention and Security

Enquiry data is stored in secure database storage with strict server-side validation and encrypted transmissions (HTTPS). We do not sell, rent, or trade customer contact details to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions regarding personal data or wish to request deletion of an enquiry record, please contact our Vadodara desk at: enquiries@aapnosaathcare.example.com.
