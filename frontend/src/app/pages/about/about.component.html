About AapnoSaath Care

Bridging the geographical distance between loving adult children and their elderly parents living in Vadodara, Gujarat.

Close to Your Parents. Wherever You Are.

"At AapnoSaath Care, we believe that living far from your parents should not mean managing every everyday responsibility alone. We aim to help families coordinate practical, local support for elderly parents while respecting their independence, preferences, and dignity.

Starting in Vadodara, our focus is on making it easier for families to arrange home visits, everyday assistance, and clear communication with the people who matter most."

Core Pillars

Our Service Philosophy

Principles that guide every conversation, home visit, and family interaction.

Respect & Independence First

We do not infantilize senior citizens or impose unwanted changes. We visit as courteous, friendly companions who listen, support agreed everyday needs, and honor the parent's daily lifestyle routines.

Consent-Driven Transparency

Family communication must be built on trust. We share post-visit updates and summaries with nominated family members with the explicit knowledge, agreement, and comfort of the elderly parent.

Clear & Honest Boundaries

We do not overpromise. AapnoSaath Care is non-medical and non-emergency. By clearly defining what we do and what we do not do, we protect families from false assumptions during critical health moments.

Deep Vadodara Local Focus

Starting in Vadodara enables us to maintain tight coordination, familiar cultural touchpoints (Gujarati conversational comfort), and local accountability rather than running a disconnected remote call center.

Initial Pilot Cohort

Our Focus on Vadodara, Gujarat

Vadodara is home to thousands of retired professionals, educators, and senior citizens whose children have pursued opportunities in North America, Europe, the Middle East, or major Indian metro hubs like Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Delhi NCR.

Our pilot initiative is intentionally centered in Vadodara to establish personal relationships, maintain consistent quality, and refine our service processes before expanding to other cities in Gujarat.
