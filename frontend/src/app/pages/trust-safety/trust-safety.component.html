Clear Operational Scope

Service Scope & Care Boundaries

AapnoSaath Care coordinates companion visits, hospital appointment accompaniment, everyday assistance, and family communication. To provide total clarity and peace of mind, our service boundaries are explicitly defined:

✓

What AapnoSaath Care DOES:

  • Friendly home check-ins and conversation
  • Local bill payments and everyday errands
  • Grocery & non-prescription supply pickups
  • Calendar reminders for appointments & medicines
  • Escorting & accompanying parents to hospital visits and doctor appointments
  • Structured visit summaries for family members
✕

What AapnoSaath Care DOES NOT Do:

  • Medical diagnosis, treatment, or therapy
  • Nursing, injections, wound care, or IV lines
  • Medication dosage adjustments or dispensing
  • 24/7 continuous ICU/bedside nursing supervision
  • Emergency medical ambulance or rescue service
  • Handling large physical cash or financial assets
In Case of Medical Emergencies: We are not an emergency responder. In acute medical crises, families and guardians in India must directly dial 108 (Ambulance) or contact nearest hospital casualty departments.
Operational Rigor

Our Safety & Verification Standards

Protocols designed to give families complete peace of mind.

Personnel Vetting

Every support coordinator undergoes government ID verification, background and reference checks, and comprehensive training in elder empathy, hospital accompaniment safety, and care boundaries before their first home visit.

Clear Identification

Coordinators arrive with verified digital and physical AapnoSaath Care credentials. Parents and families are notified in advance about who will be conducting each visit.

Elder Consent & Privacy

Visits and update-sharing proceed strictly with your parent's comfort and consent. Personal privacy is protected, and confidential family details are never shared with unauthorized third parties.

Cash & Valuables Protocols

Coordinators are prohibited from accepting personal loans, gifts, house keys, or handling large cash sums. All errand expenses are receipted and accounted for transparently.

Escalation & Communication

If a coordinator observes an unexpected situation or wellbeing concern during a visit, the designated family emergency contact is informed immediately according to our escalation protocol.

Feedback & Complaint Handling

We maintain an open channel for family and parent feedback. Any concern regarding visit conduct or coordination is reviewed promptly by the Vadodara management team.

Have Questions About Our Safety Standards?

We welcome open questions from family members. Discuss our policies directly with our coordination team.
