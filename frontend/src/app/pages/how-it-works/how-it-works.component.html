The Service Journey

How It Works

A transparent, five-step pathway ensuring dignified support for your elderly parents and continuous reassurance for your family.

1
Step 1: First Contact

Registration & Initial Enquiry

You submit an initial enquiry or book a consultation request through our website. You share basic details including your parent’s Vadodara locality, contact preferences, and the general nature of support you are seeking (companionship check-ins, routine errands, or appointment accompaniment).

No payment is collected at this stage. You receive an instant reference code to track your request.
2
Step 2: Needs Discovery

Service-Needs Assessment

Our local coordination team connects with you (via phone or video) to discuss your parent’s specific routine, preferences, language comfort (Gujarati, Hindi, English), visit frequency, and any practical assistance required.

Care Scope Clarification: This is a daily living and support-needs assessment (routine preferences, hospital accompaniment needs, mobility comfort, conversational interests), not a clinical evaluation or medical diagnosis.
3
Step 3: Mutual Agreement

Service Proposal & Booking Confirmation

You receive a written service proposal outlining the agreed visit frequency, duration (e.g., up to 60 mins), transparent pricing, cancellation terms, and scope boundaries.

Service bookings are finalized only after both your family and the parent feel comfortable with the proposed arrangement.
4
Step 4: Care In Action

Verified Service Delivery

Our assigned support coordinator visits your parents at their Vadodara home at the agreed date and time window. The coordinator carries proper identification, introduces themselves warmly, and conducts the visit following the agreed scope (conversation, tea, comfort check, or agreed errand runs).

5
Step 5: Connection & Feedback

Visit Summary & Family Update

Following each completed visit, a brief structured update is sent to the nominated family contact. This includes a general wellbeing summary, completed errands, and any observations your parents requested us to share.

Information sharing is strictly consent-driven and respects your parent's personal privacy boundaries at all times.
For Children Living Abroad

Coordinating From the USA, UK, Canada, UAE, or Other Cities?

  • Remote Consultation Scheduling: Book slots convenient for Eastern, Pacific, GMT, or Gulf Standard time zones.
  • WhatsApp & Email Summaries: Direct, clear post-visit digital updates delivered to your preferred app.
  • Single Point of Contact: A reliable local coordination team based in Vadodara to reach out to directly.
