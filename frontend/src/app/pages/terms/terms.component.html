1. Service Nature and Non-Medical Limitations

AapnoSaath Care coordinates non-medical elder care check-ins, social companionship, and practical errand coordination in Vadodara, Gujarat. AapnoSaath Care does not offer or represent itself as a medical healthcare provider, registered clinic, nursing home, or emergency rescue organization.

2. Booking Confirmation & Schedule Availability

Submission of an enquiry or consultation request on this website does not constitute a binding booking. Services are confirmed only after mutual review of needs, schedule availability, and written service plan acceptance.

3. Pricing & Third-Party Expenses

All prices displayed on the public website are proposed pilot pricing hypotheses. Specific tasks or out-of-pocket costs (such as transport fares, grocery purchases, or utility charges) are billed at actual cost and agreed beforehand.

4. Cancellations & Modifications

Visits may be rescheduled with at least 24 hours advance notice to allow route adjustments for field coordinators.
