Important Pricing Rules & Policies

We believe trust starts with complete clarity. Here is how our pricing works in practice.

Visit Durations & Extra Visits

Each visit is designed for up to 30 minutes of meaningful interaction. If additional visits or extended time are requested, we provide a clear quotation and agree before proceeding.

Third-Party Expenses Disclosed Separately

Any direct costs (such as grocery purchases, auto/cab fares, medicine bills, or government stamp duties) are paid directly by the family or reimbursed at exact cost with receipts.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Visits may be rescheduled with at least 24 hours advance notice. Our final commercial cancellation policy is currently under pilot confirmation and will be shared in writing before service activation.

Safety & Operating Boundaries

We may decline requests that fall outside safety, legality, or non-medical boundaries. We never handle large sums of physical cash or unverified banking credentials.
