Service Boundaries

Service Scope & Care Boundaries Disclaimer

Transparency on our scope, hospital accompaniment protocols, and clinical boundaries.

1. Eldercare Support & Hospital Accompaniment Scope

AapnoSaath Care provides compassionate elder-care check-ins, social companionship, hospital appointment accompaniment, and family coordination in Vadodara, Gujarat. We provide personal companion care and appointment accompaniment; we do not operate as a clinical hospital, medical clinic, nursing home, or diagnostic treatment center.

Our support coordinators accompany parents to routine hospital visits and doctor appointments, assist with transport logistics, and provide caring companionship. They are trained coordinators and companions, not licensed physicians or clinical nurses. Coordinators do not administer medical treatments, injections, IV fluids, or prescription changes. All clinical care and medical decisions remain exclusively under the supervision of qualified doctors and registered hospitals.

2. No Medical Emergency Response

AapnoSaath Care does not operate an emergency medical rescue or rapid ambulance service. In any life-threatening situation or acute medical crisis in Vadodara:

Immediately Call Emergency Medical Services:

Dial 108 (Gujarat State Emergency Ambulance) or reach out directly to the nearest hospital casualty emergency ward.

3. Third-Party Services & Financial Transactions

Where our coordinators assist in scheduling outside services (such as hospital appointments, laboratory test pickups, or local transportation), AapnoSaath Care acts solely as a coordination facilitator and assumes no clinical liability for third-party medical outcomes.
