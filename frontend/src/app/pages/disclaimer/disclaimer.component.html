Service Boundaries

Non-Medical Service Disclaimer

Transparency on our scope, limitations, and emergency medical protocols.

1. Non-Medical Support Service

AapnoSaath Care is an elder-care support, social companionship, and family coordination service operating in Vadodara, Gujarat. We are not a medical hospital, clinic, nursing agency, or medical diagnostic center.

Our support personnel are non-medical coordinators and companions. They are not authorized or qualified to perform medical examinations, dispense controlled prescription dosages, administer IV fluids or injections, or provide physical therapy or palliative clinical care.

2. No Medical Emergency Response

AapnoSaath Care does not operate an emergency medical rescue or rapid ambulance service. In any life-threatening situation or acute medical crisis in Vadodara:

Immediately Call Emergency Medical Services:

Dial 108 (Gujarat State Emergency Ambulance) or reach out directly to the nearest hospital casualty emergency ward.

3. Third-Party Services & Financial Transactions

Where our coordinators assist in scheduling outside services (such as hospital appointments, laboratory test pickups, or local transportation), AapnoSaath Care acts solely as a coordination facilitator and assumes no clinical liability for third-party medical outcomes.
